ARVION DARTS 3D — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026. This policy applies to ARVION DARTS 3D for Wear OS watches, package com.aplikacjeandroid.arviondarts3d.

Developer and contact

Developer: Łukasz Bednarski / ARVION. For privacy questions, contact obdactiveexhaust@gmail.com.

Local game data

The game stores your current match, recent turn history, scores, hit statistics, local achievements, selected darts, language and settings in the application's private device storage. There is no game account, online leaderboard, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated data collection server. The developer does not receive your game data.

Trial and device identifier

The 60-minute trial runs from installation, including time while the game is closed. To detect clock rollback and changes to trial state, the game processes installation time, the highest device time it has observed, elapsed system time and signed local trial checkpoints. Android's app-scoped ANDROID_ID is used only locally as part of the trial integrity signature; it is not sent to the developer or used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Client-side checks cannot guarantee resistance to rooted devices, complete data removal or every reinstall.

Purchases

The game offers one permanent, non-consumable Google Play purchase. There are no subscriptions. Google Play processes payments and account/purchase information according to Google's policies. The app receives product identifiers, purchase state, purchase token and purchase signature to validate, acknowledge and restore access. These are processed on the device and through the Google Play Billing service; the developer does not receive payment-card details. A local entitlement cache enables offline use after validation. A successful Google Play restore can update that cache, including removal of a refunded or revoked entitlement. Restoring on another device requires the same Google Play account and the same app/product listing.

Network and external services

Gameplay and local statistics work offline. Purchases, restoration and app distribution depend on Google Play and may require connectivity. Opening this privacy policy uses an external browser or system handler. The policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard web access information when you visit it. See Google Privacy Policy and GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Android backup and transfer

Where enabled by your device and account settings, Android backup or device transfer can include game statistics, settings and signed trial checkpoints. The purchase cache and active match are excluded from the app's declared backup rules. Google or your device provider handles backup under their policies. A restored trial checkpoint can fail integrity verification on a different device and require restoring a valid purchase.

Permissions

The app declares Google Play Billing, vibration, Internet and network-state permissions and requires OpenGL ES 2.0. Network permissions support Google Play services; gameplay remains local. It does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location, health data or broad file access.

Retention, deletion and security

Data remains locally until you clear the application's storage or uninstall it. Recent match history is limited in the interface and local archive. Removing local game data does not cancel or erase Google's purchase records. Device/cloud backup copies follow your Android backup settings. To manage purchase or backup records, use your Google account controls. Private app storage and trial integrity checks reduce casual tampering but cannot guarantee security on a compromised device. There is no developer-held game account to delete.

Children and changes

The app has no chat, social profiles or advertising and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. This statement does not replace the age/content rating or target audience selected by the publisher in Google Play. Future changes to data practices will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
